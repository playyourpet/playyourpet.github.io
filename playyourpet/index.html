How it works

From photo to pet in a minute

Pick a photo

Take a new one or choose one from your gallery. Pets, people, plants, toys, snacks. If you love it, it counts.

Make it a pet

Crop it, erase the background and frame the face in the built-in editor. Give it a name and, if you like, a voice.

Play

Your pet is the hero of all 20 mini games, and between rounds it lives in the Pet Home.

Pet Home

A home between rounds

Your pet has a kitchen, a bathroom, a living room and a bedroom. Feed it, give it a bath, dress it up and tuck it in at night.

  • Hats, glasses and more from the wardrobe
  • Watch it grow on the age map
  • Photo mode for saving and sharing the good moments
  • Daily rewards, quests and badges
Make it yours

Change (almost) everything

Most of what you see in the games can be swapped for something of yours.

  • Your photos as characters, objects and backgrounds
  • Your own sounds: record one, or take it from any audio or video file
  • Several pets, and you choose who plays
  • In Voice Pet, your voice is the controller
Ads and purchases

Plain rules for ads

Play Your Pet is free. Ads and optional purchases pay for it. Here's exactly when you'll see an ad, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Why we chose these rules

  1. No full-screen ads in your first 24 hours or your first 8 games.
  2. After that, one can appear only when a mini game ends, on the results screen. Never mid-game, never in the Pet Home or the menus.
  3. At most one every 5 to 10 games, with at least two minutes of play in between, and no more than 10 a day.
  4. Reward videos are always your choice. Watch one for a bonus, or don't.
  5. Buy Remove Ads once, or make any real-money purchase, and full-screen ads are gone.
Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone

The photos and voice recordings you add are stored on your device and never uploaded. No account, no sign-up.

The game uses Google and Unity services for ads, purchases, usage statistics and crash reports. Our privacy policy lists what each one collects and how to delete it.
