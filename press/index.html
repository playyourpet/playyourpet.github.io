Play Your Pet, one line

Anything can be your pet: add it with one photo, then play 20 mini games with it.

Play Your Pet, one paragraph

Play Your Pet turns anything into the star of 20 mini games. Take a photo of your cat, your dog, your best friend or your uncle, trim it in the built-in editor and it's in the game: flying, fishing, climbing and stacking its way through arcade, timing, puzzle and action games. Between rounds it lives in a Pet Home where players feed it, bathe it, dress it up and watch it grow. Photos and voice recordings stay on the player's phone and are never uploaded.

About Zaxlon Games

Zaxlon Games is an independent mobile game studio making games that are quick to learn and easy to make your own. Its first game, Play Your Pet, launched worldwide on Google Play in November 2026 in 20 languages. The studio's mascot, Zax, is an axolotl, an animal that can regrow what it loses.

Brand basics

Colors and a few rules

Our logo and Zax are set in Nunito, with thick ink outlines and flat color. No gradients.

Ink#15161B
Cream#FFF4EA

Please do

  • Use the logo on cream, white or ink backgrounds.
  • Leave clear space around it, at least the height of the Z.
  • Use Zax's expressions as they are, at any size.

Please don't

  • Stretch, recolor, outline or rotate the logo.
  • Put Zax in another logo, or on products for sale.
  • Make Zax purple. Zax is not a purple axolotl.
