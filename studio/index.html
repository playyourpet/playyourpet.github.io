We make games
you make your own.

Zaxlon Games is an independent mobile game studio. We make games that are quick to pick up, a little silly, and easy to make your own.

We started with one question: what if the game was about your pet, not ours?

Most pet games give you a cartoon animal and ask you to love it. We flipped that around. You bring the pet, and the game builds itself around it. That idea became Play Your Pet, a collection of 20 mini games where the hero is whatever you take a photo of.

We like ideas that sound odd at first, like a game you steer by shouting. And we keep listening after launch: when players tell us something's off, we fix it fast.

Everything ships in 20 languages, because a kid in Jakarta and a grandparent in São Paulo should both be able to read the menu.

How we make games

Four things we care about

01

Easy to start

If a game needs a tutorial longer than a sentence, we keep working on it. You should be playing within seconds.

02

Losing is part of the fun

Zax can regrow a lost limb, so a lost round is no big deal around here. Fail, laugh, tap again.

03

Your stuff stays yours

Photos and recordings you add stay on your phone. We never see them, and you don't need an account to play.

04

Made for everywhere

Our games ship in 20 languages, from Turkish to Japanese, and they run on older phones too.

Meet Zax

Our mascot is an axolotl

Axolotls can regrow lost limbs, and even parts of their heart. We like that as a way to think about games: you lose, you grow back, you go again. Zax holds on to the Z of Zaxlon and has more faces than we can count. Here are ten.
