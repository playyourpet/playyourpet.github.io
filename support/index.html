Getting started

Is Play Your Pet free?

Yes. It's free to download and play. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases, like coin packs and a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.1 or later.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Play Your Pet is only on Android for now. If you'd like it on iPhone, tell us: every request helps us decide. If it happens, we'll announce it here first.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your phone.

Your pet

How do I add my pet?

The first time you open the game, it asks you to choose a pet. You can pick one of the ready-made pets or add your own. Later, you can add more with the Add Your Pet button on each game's start screen.

Choose a photo or take a new one, then crop it, erase the background and frame the face in the editor.

Does it have to be an animal?

Not at all. Friends, family, toys, plants, food. If it fits in a photo, it can be your pet.

Can my pet have its own sound?

Yes. Record a sound, or pick one from any audio or video file on your phone.

I moved to a new phone and my pet's photo is gone.

Your progress can come back with Android's backup, but photos and recordings stay on the old phone, because we never upload them. The game will ask you to add your pet's photo again.

Purchases

I paid for something but didn't get it.

Close the game completely and open it again while you're connected to the internet. Unfinished orders are delivered the next time the game starts.

If it's still missing after that, email us your Google Play order number. It starts with GPA. and you'll find it in the receipt Google sent you.

How do I get Remove Ads or Coin Booster back after reinstalling?

These are tied to your Google account. Install the game on a phone signed in to the same Google account and they come back automatically.

Coin packs are used up when you buy them, so they can't be restored.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments are handled by Google Play, so refunds go through Google. Their help page explains how: Get refunds on Google Play purchases.

Ads

When do ads show up?

Full-screen ads only appear on the results screen after a mini game ends. None in your first 24 hours or first 8 games, at most one every 5 to 10 games, and no more than 10 a day. Never in the middle of a game, in the Pet Home or in the menus.

The full rules are here.

How do I turn ads off?

Buy Remove Ads once, or make any other real-money purchase, and full-screen ads stop. Reward videos stay available, but they only play when you choose to watch one.

I saw an ad that wasn't appropriate.

Sorry about that. Email us which game you were in, roughly when it happened and, if you remember, what the ad was for. We can block specific advertisers and whole categories.

Settings

How do I turn off the music or sound effects?

Open Settings from the main menu. Music and sound effects each have their own switch and volume slider. Vibration is there too.

How do I change the language?

The game starts in your phone's language if it's one of the 20 we support. You can change it any time in Settings.

Can I turn off notifications?

Yes, in Settings. The game uses notifications for things like daily rewards, and on Android 13 and later it asks for your permission first.

Privacy

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos and voice recordings you add stay on your device. The game does use Google and Unity services for ads, purchases, usage statistics and crash reports. Our privacy policy lists what each one collects.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your phone. For data held by the services we use, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

Problems

The game crashes or won't start.

Make sure the game is up to date in Google Play and that your phone has some free storage, then restart your phone. If it still happens, email us your phone model and Android version.

Voice Pet doesn't hear me.

Voice Pet needs the microphone. If you said no when the game asked, open your phone's Settings, go to Apps, then Play Your Pet, then Permissions, and allow the microphone.
